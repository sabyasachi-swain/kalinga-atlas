Climb down to the docks! Ports like these once buzzed with sailors, traders, and cargo unloaded from faraway ships.

Archaeologists who dug at the port of Manikapatna agree it once traded with distant lands. They found broken pottery and coins from places as far away as China and Sri Lanka [Tripati, 2021, p.1212; Patra, n.d., pp.111-112]. Tap a port below to see what else turned up in the ground.
