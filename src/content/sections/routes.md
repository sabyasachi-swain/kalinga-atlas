title: Trade Routes

Step aboard! For over two thousand years, ships and carts carried goods in and out of Kalinga. Some paths followed the coast. Others crossed rivers or went inland to far-off cities.

A Chinese monk named Xuanzang once visited a busy port in Kalinga. He wrote that traders leaving from it sailed away to far lands [Xuanzang, 7th century CE, p.205]. Look at the routes below. Picture the ships and travelers who once used them.
