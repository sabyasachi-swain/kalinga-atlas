Step aboard! One historian thinks a road may have linked the port of Palur to the inland town of Jaugada, over two thousand years ago [Patra, n.d., p.113]. Centuries later, boats still carried goods along a river canal from Cuttack to the coast [Imperial Gazetteer of India (Orissa entries), n.d., p.92].

A Chinese monk named Xuanzang wrote about a busy port on this coast. He wrote that traders leaving from it sailed away to far lands [Xuanzang, 7th century CE, p.205]. Look at the routes below. Picture the ships and travelers who once used them.
