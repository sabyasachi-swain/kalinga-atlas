What did the ships carry? Salt was once an important export, sent inland along a great road beside the Mahanadi river [Stirling, 1825, p.194]. An old book on statecraft named Kalinga's cotton cloth among the finest in India [Kautilya, n.d., p.84].

An old book about ruling a land said Kalinga had some of the best elephants in India [Kautilya, n.d., p.49]. A Chinese traveler agreed, years later. He said nearby lands prized these elephants too [Xuanzang, 7th century CE, p.207]. Look below to meet the goods that moved in and out of Kalinga.
