An 1872 book says molasses was one of the goods carried down the Mahanadi river from Sambalpur to the coast. Boats swapped it there for salt and other goods going the other way. [Hunter, 1872, vol. II Appendix III, p. 74]

## For scholars

Only one source documents this, so the tier stops at Probable. The passage describes river and inland trade only. No source here says molasses reached the sea or left India. [Hunter, 1872, vol. II Appendix III, p. 74]
