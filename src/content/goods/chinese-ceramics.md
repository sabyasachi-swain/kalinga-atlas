title: Chinese ceramics
---

Diggers at the old port of Manikapatna found pieces of Chinese pottery in the sand. They found green celadon ware and blue-and-white porcelain. They even found a coin from a Chinese emperor [Tripati, 2021, p.1212].

At another old port, Khalkatapatna, archaeologists found the same kinds of pottery. They also found two small Chinese copper coins from around the 14th century [Patra, 2014, pp.119-120].

Historians agree this pottery crossed thousands of miles of ocean. It landed on these shores long ago. The ships that carried it are long gone [Patra, n.d., pp.111-112].

## For scholars
The Khalkatapatna pottery dates from about the 12th to 14th century. Manikapatna's porcelain dates later, from 1368 to 1644. Finding Chinese pots does not prove Chinese ships sailed here. The goods may have passed through Southeast Asian traders first. Two of the four reports used here share the same author.
