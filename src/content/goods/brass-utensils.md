An 1872 book says river towns carried brass pots and pans up the Mahanadi river to Sambalpur. They probably travelled on the same boats as salt, spices and coconuts. [Hunter, 1872, vol. II Appendix III, p. 74]

## For scholars

Only one source documents this, so the tier stops at Probable. Hunter does not say where the brass utensils were made. They moved from the coast inland, but nothing here proves they were made in Odisha, or shows a sea route. [Hunter, 1872, vol. II Appendix III, p. 74]
