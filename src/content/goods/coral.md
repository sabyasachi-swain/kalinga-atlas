An 1825 British survey says a small amount of coral reached the Odisha coast. It came on ships from the Maldive Islands, along with cowrie shells, coconuts and dried fish. [Stirling, 1825, p. 194]

## For scholars

Only one source documents this: Stirling's 1825 survey. It gives no quantity or price beyond "a small supply". No source here says what the coral was used for once it reached Odisha. [Stirling, 1825, p. 194]
