Ghee is clarified butter. Boats carried pots of ghee down the Mahanadi river. They went from Sambalpur to the coast. [Hunter, 1872, vol. II Appendix III, p. 74]

An 1872 book also names ghee as part of everyday trade. Inland areas called the Tributary States sold it. Historians think ghee was a normal trade good there. [Hunter, 1872, vol. II, p. 102]

## For scholars

Both references come from the same book, by Hunter. So only one source supports this entry. That keeps the tier at Probable. Neither passage says the ghee reached the sea. Neither says it left India. [Hunter, 1872, vol. II Appendix III, p. 74; Hunter, 1872, vol. II, p. 102]
