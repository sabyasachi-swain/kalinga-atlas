title: Semi-precious stone beads
---

Diggers at the port of Palur report finding tiny beads. They are made of carnelian, agate, jasper and quartz. Some are made of faience too, a glassy material with a shiny glaze. This find comes from a dig that is not yet fully published. So far it is reported only at a conference and in a newspaper [Sahu, 2026, Times of India, 1 August 2026].

The beads turned up next to pottery from many different centuries. That pottery spans from before the Mauryan period to the early medieval period. So nobody yet knows exactly when the beads themselves were made [Sahu, 2026, Times of India, 1 August 2026].

It is also not known where the beads were made. They may have been made right at Palur. Or they may have arrived there as finished ornaments [Sahu, 2026, Times of India, 1 August 2026].
