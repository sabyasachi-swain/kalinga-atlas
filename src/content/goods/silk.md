An 1825 British survey says silk cloth came into Cuttack from nearby districts of Bengal. This was a different, finer cloth. Odisha's own rough tasar silk travelled the other way. [Stirling, 1825, p. 194]

## For scholars

Only one source documents this import. Stirling does not say what kind of silk it was, or how much arrived. Do not confuse this with the tasar-silk entry. That is Odisha's own wild silk, moving in the opposite direction. [Stirling, 1825, p. 194]
