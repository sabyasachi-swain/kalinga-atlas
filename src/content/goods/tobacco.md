An 1825 British survey says good tobacco came into Cuttack. It came from nearby districts of Bengal. It arrived with cloth and silk, as a luxury good. [Stirling, 1825, p. 194]

## For scholars

Only one source records this import. It gives no quantity. [Stirling, 1825, p. 194]
