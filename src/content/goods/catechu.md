An 1825 British survey lists "kutha" among the goods taxed on leaving Cuttack. Kutha is the thick, dried juice of the khair tree. People used it for tanning leather and for dyeing. [Stirling, 1825, p. 194]

## For scholars

Only one source documents this, so the tier stops at Probable. Stirling's list names goods taxed on leaving Cuttack district. That is not proof of a sea export. [Stirling, 1825, p. 194]
