River boats carried bright yellow turmeric down the Mahanadi river. They went from Sambalpur to the coast. Traders swapped it there for salt, spices and brass pots. [Hunter, 1872, vol. II Appendix III, p. 74]

An 1872 book lists turmeric too. It names turmeric among everyday goods. Rice, sugar-cane and cotton went along with it. Historians think turmeric was a normal trade good inland. [Hunter, 1872, vol. II, p. 102]

## For scholars

Both passages come from the same book, by Hunter. So only one source supports this entry. That keeps the tier at Probable. Neither passage says the turmeric reached the sea. Neither says it left India. [Hunter, 1872, vol. II Appendix III, p. 74; Hunter, 1872, vol. II, p. 102]
