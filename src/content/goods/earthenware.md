An 1825 British survey says ships from the Maldive Islands filled up on rice and clay pots. They did this every time they visited Balasore and Dhamra, on the Odisha coast. [Stirling, 1825, p. 194]

## For scholars

Only one source documents this, so the tier stops at Probable. Stirling does not describe the pots. He gives no size, shape or use. He does not say where in Odisha they were made. [Stirling, 1825, p. 194]
