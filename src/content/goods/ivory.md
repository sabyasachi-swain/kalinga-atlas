title: Ivory
---

Long ago, a Greek sea captain wrote a guide for sailors. He named a coast Dosarene. He said it gave its name to a fine ivory: "Dosarenic ivory," cut from elephant tusks [Anonymous, 1989, section 62].

Historians think Dosarene lay on the Odisha coast. It may be near Chilika lake. No dig has found the town yet. The name is still a puzzle [Patra, 2014, p.122].

Traders across the ancient world wanted ivory. They carved it into combs and bangles. This coast became known for its ivory. But no ivory workshop has ever been found here [Patra, 2014, p.122].

## For scholars
No dig has found a worked tusk here. "Dosarene" is not the same word as "Kalinga." Three scholars have placed it in three different spots: near Toshali, near Palur, and near Chilika lake. Until a dig finds ivory, this stays a probable link. It is not a proven one.
