Jaugada is a ruined fort in south Odisha. It stands on the bank of the Rushikulya river. Inside it, a huge slab of granite carries thirteen of Ashoka's edicts. They were cut about 250 BCE [The Imperial Gazetteer of India, new edition (Oxford, Clarendon Press, 1907-09), 1908, p.72].

Old pots, tiles and coins lie scattered inside the fort's earth wall. Archaeologists found a full iron-using culture here. They also found beads of shell, bone, agate and crystal, plus old punch-marked coins [Patra, n.d., p.109].
