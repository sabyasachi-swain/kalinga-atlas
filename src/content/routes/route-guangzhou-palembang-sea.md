The Chinese monk Yijing sailed from the port of Guangzhou to Palembang, on his way to study in India. He wrote down his own voyage, stop by stop. [Dayalan, 2019, offprint p. 27]

## For scholars

Only one source was read here. So the tier stops at Probable. This rests on a retelling of an old 1894 translation, not an independent primary reading. No cargo is named. This was a monk's pilgrimage trip. The sea lane shown is schematic. [Dayalan, 2019, offprint p. 27]
