Dhamra was the older port at this river mouth. A government order made it official in 1858. Old records rank it right after Balasore among Odisha's harbours. [Hunter, 1872, vol. ii, Appendix IV, pp. 107-108]

Chandbali grew up further inland, on the same estuary. It went on to take over most of Dhamra's trade. [District Gazetteers (Cuttack, Puri, Ganjam, Balasore), British-era editions, n.d., O'Malley, Bengal District Gazetteers: Balasore (1907), p. 8]

## For scholars

Both records describe a shift in trade over time. Dhamra was named a port in 1858. Chandbali, 20 miles further up the same river, later drew its trade away. Neither source describes boats sailing this exact stretch, so the line and any goods shown here are schematic. Neither source says when the shift was complete.
