Sonapur and Barua were small ports further south, on the same old coast. The ancient Greek geographer Ptolemy wrote about places like Barua. He called it an important trade centre. Historians think ships probably moved between these close, neighbouring ports. [Patra, 2014, pp. 118, 121-123]

## For scholars

No voyage or record between these two specific ports was found. The route is inferred, from how close they sit, about 35 km apart, and from a general model of coastal hopping. Both port identifications trace back to a 1918 district manual, which was not read directly in this session. The line shown is schematic. [Patra, 2014, pp. 118, 121-123]
