Manikapatna and Khalkatapatna were busy ports on the Odisha coast, about 800 years ago. Digs at Khalkatapatna found Chinese celadon pottery. They also found porcelain with a blue flower pattern. Digs at Manikapatna found rouletted ware and broken Roman jars. A later layer there held Chinese pottery too. [Patra, 2014, pp. 119-120]

Another dig report found similar things. Chinese celadon, porcelain, and even a Chinese coin turned up at Manikapatna. These match finds at Khalkatapatna. [Patnaik, 2014, p. 105]

## For scholars

The link between these two ports is shared Chinese pottery. It is not a written record of ships sailing between them. So the tier stops at Probable. The two sites sit about 50 km apart along the coast. The line drawn here is schematic, along the modern shore. Most sources date Khalkatapatna's Chinese finds to the 1100s-1300s CE. That fits a later era than the full Somavamshi period tagged here. [Patra, 2014, pp. 119-120; Patnaik, 2014, p. 105]
