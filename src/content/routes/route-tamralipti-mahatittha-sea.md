A 5th-century Buddhist book, the Samantapasadika, says ships often sailed between Tamralipti and Mahatittha, a great port in Sri Lanka. [Dayalan, 2019, offprint p. 24]

## For scholars

Only one source was read here. So the tier stops at Probable. This claim is a retelling of an old Pali text, passed through other scholars' work. It is not an independent reading of the original commentary. No cargo is named. The sea lane shown is a schematic line down the Bay of Bengal, not a plotted sailing course. [Dayalan, 2019, offprint p. 24]
