Balugaon was a fishing and grain town on the edge of Chilika lake. A 1929 gazetteer says most of the lake's fish and grain traffic passed through Balugaon and a nearby spot, Kalupara Ghat. [District Gazetteers (Cuttack, Puri, Ganjam, Balasore), British-era editions, n.d., O'Malley & Mansfield, Bihar and Orissa District Gazetteers: Puri (1929), pp. 204-205, 212-213]

## For scholars

Only one source was read here. So the tier stops at Probable. This gazetteer records Balugaon's fish and grain trade, and its later rail link. It does not describe a specific boat route to Puri.
