Barua and Kalingapatnam both sat where small rivers meet the sea. Kalingapatnam sits at the mouth of the Vamsadhara river. It was the bigger of the two ports. Historians think ships probably moved between these two neighbours, along the same coast. [Patra, 2014, pp. 118, 123]

## For scholars

No voyage or record between these two ports was found. The route is inferred, from how close they sit, about 75 km apart, and from a general model of coastal hopping. The line shown is schematic, drawn along the modern coast. [Patra, 2014, pp. 118, 123]
