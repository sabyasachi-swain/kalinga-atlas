Palur and Sonapur were both old ports on the south Odisha coast. Historians think ships probably hopped down the coast from one small port to the next, since these ports sit only about 55 km apart. [Patra, 2014, pp. 118, 121-122]

## For scholars

No voyage or record between these two named ports was found. The route is inferred, from how close they sit and from a general model of coastal hopping between neighbouring ports. Sonapur's own date rests only on this source's general framing of "ports of ancient Odisha." No dig or inscription dates it directly. The line shown is schematic, drawn along the modern coast. [Patra, 2014, pp. 118, 121-122]
