After Palembang, the monk Yijing's ship carried him on to Kedah, on the Malay Peninsula. Old records say he "proceeded northwest to Kie-tcha" on this leg of his voyage. [Dayalan, 2019, offprint p. 27]

## For scholars

Only one source was read here. So the tier stops at Probable. This rests on a retelling of an old 1894 translation, not an independent primary reading. Yijing's account also names stops at Mo-lo-yu and in Sumatra, between Palembang and Kedah. Those places are not modelled as separate ports in this atlas, so this line skips them. The sea lane shown is schematic. [Dayalan, 2019, offprint p. 27]
