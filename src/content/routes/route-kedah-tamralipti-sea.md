From Kedah, a Chinese monk named Yijing sailed on. Old records say he "proceeded east and reached the Nicobar Islands," then turned northwest and reached Tamralipti, the great port at the top of the Bay of Bengal. On his way home, he sailed the same route again. [Dayalan, 2019, offprint p. 27]

## For scholars

Only one source was read here. So the tier stops at Probable. This rests on a retelling of an old 1894 translation, not a direct check of the original text. The Nicobar Islands are not treated as a port in this atlas. So the stop shown there is schematic, read off a modern map. Yijing made this same trip in reverse on his way home. One entry stands here for both directions. [Dayalan, 2019, offprint p. 27]
