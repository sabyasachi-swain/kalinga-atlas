Chandbali was a small port on the Baitarani river. From here, a steamer sailed up the coast to Calcutta three times every week. It also ran by canal from Cuttack down to Chandbali. It carried people. It carried goods too. [Imperial Gazetteer of India (Orissa entries), n.d., p.92]

Chandbali's ships did not stop at Calcutta. They also sailed west, near Bombay. They sailed east too, all the way to Arakan. Some ships went even further. They reached the Maldive Islands. They reached the island of Ceylon as well. Calcutta kept growing bigger under British rule. Slowly, it pulled trade away from small ports like Chandbali. [District Gazetteers (Cuttack, Puri, Ganjam, Balasore), British-era editions, n.d., p.144]

## For scholars

No sailing chart was read for this route. So the line drawn on the map is just a rough guess, not a path any ship truly sailed. [Imperial Gazetteer of India (Orissa entries), n.d., p.92]
