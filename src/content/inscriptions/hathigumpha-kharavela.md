Hathigumpha means "Elephant Cave." It is a cave cut into a hill near Bhubaneswar, in Odisha. Long lines are carved on its wall. They tell the story of a king named Kharavela [Sircar, 1965, p.213].

The words tell of gifts sent from far away. A king in the south sent elephants, jewels, and pearls. Scholars care about this cave because it holds Kharavela's own words. But the rock has big cracks. Some words are hard to read. Two scholars have read some lines in very different ways, so no one line is fully settled [Sircar, 1965, p.217].

## For scholars

Even the cave's age is debated. One study says the early 100s BCE. A later study says the end of the 100s BCE, about a hundred years after that [Sircar, 1965, p.213]. One damaged line matters most for trade. Early readers thought it named a port town. A later scholar read different letters there. He said the port-town reading cannot be trusted. Until the rock is read again, neither guess should be called the truth [Sircar, 1965, p.217].
