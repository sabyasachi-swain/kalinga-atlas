title: Ashoka's Separate Rock Edicts at Jaugada
---

The same two royal messages that Ashoka carved at Dhauli were also cut at Jaugada. Jaugada is an old fort by the Rishikulya river. It is in Ganjam district, Odisha. Here, the messages are for officers in a town called Samapa [Hultzsch, 1925, p.117].

Historians agree this fixes a second Mauryan town on the Odisha coast. Diggers at Jaugada also found old coins, pottery, and an earthen wall [Patra, n.d., p.109].

One historian thinks the messages were placed here because of a nearby port called Palur. That is just his own idea. The messages say nothing about ports, ships, or goods [Patra, n.d., p.113].

## For scholars

Much of the Jaugada rock has crumbled away. About half of the first message is gone. About a third of the second message is gone too. Those words can never be read again [Hultzsch, 1925, p.14].

The port-of-Palur idea is a guess. It is not something written in the stone. Readers should not treat it as settled fact [Patra, n.d., p.113].
