Arikamedu is an old trading village on the Ariyankuppam river. It sits close to modern Puducherry, in south India. Long ago, it may have been the port called "Poduke" in an old Greek sailing guide. [Dayalan, 2019, p.12]

Diggers found the same shiny, stamped pots here as at Kalinga's ports. Those ports are Manikapatna and Sisupalgarh. This pottery is called rouletted ware. Historians think it links Arikamedu and Kalinga in one shared trade web. That web was busy around two thousand years ago. [Patra, n.d., p.111; Dayalan, 2019, p.12]

No record of a real trip between Arikamedu and Kalinga has been found. So historians are careful here. They call this a shared trade web. It was probably built on many short trips, not one straight sea route. [Patra, n.d., p.110; Dayalan, 2019, p.12]

## For scholars

The Kalinga link rests only on matching pots. No written record shows ships sailing between the two coasts. [Patra, n.d., p.111; Dayalan, 2019, p.12]
