Kedah was a stopping place on the Malay Peninsula. A Chinese Buddhist monk named Yijing passed through it. He was sailing on his way to India. Old records say he went "northwest to Kie-tcha." Then he sailed on, past the Nicobar Islands, to Tamralipti. On his way home, he landed at the same place again. [Dayalan, 2019, offprint p. 27]

## For scholars

Only one source was read here. So the tier stops at Probable. Scholars usually match this old name, Kie-tcha, to the Bujang Valley area of Kedah, Malaysia today. That reading comes from Dayalan's retelling of an old 1894 translation. It is not a direct check of the original text. No source here gives a coordinate. The position given is a rough guess at the Bujang Valley dig area, read from a map. [Dayalan, 2019, offprint p. 27]
