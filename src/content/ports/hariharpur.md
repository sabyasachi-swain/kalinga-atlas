Hariharpur was a small trading post in the middle of the Mahanadi river delta. Ships could not reach it directly; it stood on a channel a little way inland. [NEEDS VERIFICATION] <!-- fact-check: no source says this. The same record has a ship lying off the town in 1633. --> [District Gazetteers (Cuttack, Puri, Ganjam, Balasore), British-era editions, n.d., p.34; Dayalan, 2019, p.23]

In 1633, English traders built a house of business here. They hoped to reach ships in the delta's channels. [District Gazetteers (Cuttack, Puri, Ganjam, Balasore), British-era editions, n.d., p.35; Dayalan, 2019, p.23]

Life there was hard. Fever made many of them sick. Five of the six traders died in a short time. The English soon gave up the post. [District Gazetteers (Cuttack, Puri, Ganjam, Balasore), British-era editions, n.d., p.35; Dayalan, 2019, p.23]

Historians think Hariharpur probably worked as a small river landing for a few years, more than a busy port. [NEEDS VERIFICATION] <!-- fact-check: the page we cite does not say this. It lists the town as a busy port. --> [Dayalan, 2019, p.23]
