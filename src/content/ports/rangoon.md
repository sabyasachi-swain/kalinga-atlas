Rangoon, now called Yangon, is a busy river port in Burma. It sits about 21 miles from the sea, across open water from the Kalinga coast. [The Imperial Gazetteer of India, new edition (Oxford, Clarendon Press, 1907-09), 1908, p.213]

A hundred years ago, ships sailed there often from the ports of Ganjam, in south Odisha. Many passengers travelled to Rangoon and back again each year. [The Imperial Gazetteer of India, new edition (Oxford, Clarendon Press, 1907-09), 1908, p.152]

No source read here names an old Burmese port tied to Kalinga in ancient times. So this entry tells only Rangoon's colonial-era story. [The Imperial Gazetteer of India, new edition (Oxford, Clarendon Press, 1907-09), 1908, p.152]

## For scholars

Both facts about British-era Rangoon come from the same Imperial Gazetteer. So they count as one source of proof. That keeps this entry at Probable. [The Imperial Gazetteer of India, new edition (Oxford, Clarendon Press, 1907-09), 1908, p.213]
