Guangzhou is a great port city in south China. More than a thousand years ago, a Chinese Buddhist monk named Yijing sailed from here. He was on his way to India by sea, and old records say he passed Fo-che, Mo-lo-yu and Sumatra along the way. He took the same route home again. [Dayalan, 2019, offprint p. 27]

## For scholars

Only a single passing mention was read here. It names Guangzhou as Yijing's starting point, not a description of the port itself. So this entry rests on very thin evidence, and the tier stops at Probable. No source here gives a coordinate. The position given is the modern city of Guangzhou, read from a map. Guangzhou stands for the Chinese end of the sea route to India used by Buddhist pilgrims. [Dayalan, 2019, offprint p. 27]
