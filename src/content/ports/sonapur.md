Sonapur was an old port in south Odisha. It sat where the Bahuda river reaches the sea. Historians think ships once loaded up on oysters here. Sand bars later blocked the way in. The port then lost its importance. [Patra, 2014, pp. 121-122]

## For scholars

Only one source was read here. So the tier stops at Probable. It gives no inscription, text or dig date for Sonapur itself. Other ports in this atlas have that kind of proof; Sonapur does not.
