Barua was a port that the ancient Greek geographer Ptolemy wrote about. It stood where a small river reaches the sea. The spot is now in Andhra Pradesh, near the modern town of Sompeta. Historians think Barua was an important trade centre in ancient times. [Patra, 2014, p. 123]
