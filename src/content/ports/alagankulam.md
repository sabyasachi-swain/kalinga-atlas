Alagankulam is a very old port. It sits on the Vaigai river in Tamil Nadu, close to the sea. Diggers found broken Roman jars there, called amphorae. They also found a lot of stamped pottery. They found real Roman coins too, made for the emperors Valentinian, Theodosius I and Arcadius I. [Dayalan, 2019, offprint p. 6, section 'Alagankulam']

## For scholars

Only one source was read here. So the tier stops at Probable. The original dig reports were not read directly. Some scholars link this port to a place named by Ptolemy. Others link it to an old Tamil port called Caliyur. These are ideas, not proven facts. Alagankulam is not a Kalinga port. It is a Bay of Bengal trading partner, with dated Roman coin finds. [Dayalan, 2019, offprint p. 6, section 'Alagankulam']
