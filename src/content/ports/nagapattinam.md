Nagapattinam is an old port town on the Tamil Nadu coast. Digs there, between 1856 and 1934, found as many as 350 Buddhist bronze statues. They date from the 800s to the 1600s CE. Old copper plates say a king of Srivijaya, a sea kingdom based in Sumatra, built a Buddhist shrine here. A Chola king named Rajaraja I gave his permission. [Dayalan, 2019, offprint pp. 7-8, section 'Nagappattinam']
