Palembang was the great capital of Srivijaya, a sea kingdom based in Sumatra. About thirteen hundred years ago, a Chinese Buddhist monk named Yijing sailed there. Old records say he "left the Guang-Zhou region ... visited Fo-che, Mo-lo-yu and Sumatra," then sailed on towards India. On his way home, he stopped at the same port again. [Dayalan, 2019, offprint p. 27]

## For scholars

Only one source was read here. So the tier stops at Probable. Scholars usually match this old name, Fo-che, to Srivijaya's capital, at modern Palembang. That reading follows a retelling of an old 1894 translation. It is not a direct check of the original Chinese or French text. No source here gives a coordinate. The position given is the modern city of Palembang, read from a map. [Dayalan, 2019, offprint p. 27]
