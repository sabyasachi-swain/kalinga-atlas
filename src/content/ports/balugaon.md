Balugaon is a town on the shore of Chilika lake. A 1929 gazetteer says the District Fishery Office moved here from Puri. Most fish and grain on the lake moved through Balugaon and a nearby spot called Kalupara Ghat. [District Gazetteers (Cuttack, Puri, Ganjam, Balasore), British-era editions, n.d., O'Malley & Mansfield, Bihar and Orissa District Gazetteers: Puri (1929), pp. 204-205, 212-213]

The same gazetteer names Balugaon as a busy stop on the main railway line. It ranked high for both passengers and goods. Historians think the railway let Chilika's fish and grain reach faraway markets. [District Gazetteers (Cuttack, Puri, Ganjam, Balasore), British-era editions, n.d., same, p. 220]

## For scholars

Only one source was read here. So the tier stops at Probable. This gazetteer shows Balugaon as a fishery office and rail goods point. It sits on Chilika lake. It was not a sea-going harbour. Its trade moved by lake boat and by rail. No record from before the 1900s was found for Balugaon. No source prints a coordinate for the town. Its position is read off a modern map, at today's railway station. [District Gazetteers (Cuttack, Puri, Ganjam, Balasore), British-era editions, n.d., O'Malley & Mansfield, Bihar and Orissa District Gazetteers: Puri (1929), pp. 204-205, 212-213]
